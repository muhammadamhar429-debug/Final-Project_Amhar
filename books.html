Formats & pricing

Secondhand pricing depends on condition; the ranges below cover most of what's on the shelf. New releases are sold at the publisher's cover price.

Selling your own books

We buy secondhand books in good condition, in person, no appointment needed. Bring a box to the counter and one of us will look through it while you browse — it usually takes about ten minutes. Details and current buying limits are on the Contact page.
