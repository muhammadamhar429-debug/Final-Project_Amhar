About Thistledown

Three rooms, one till, and a lot of opinions about what to read next.

How it started

Thistledown Books opened in a former print shop on Mill Lane. The building had housed a newspaper press for sixty years before that, and the floor still slopes slightly toward where the machines once stood — we left it that way.

The shop began as a single room of secondhand paperbacks. New titles were added once regular customers started asking for them by name, and the second room — now Fiction and Poetry — opened two years later when the back storage space was cleared out.

What we stock

We split roughly evenly between new releases and secondhand copies. Staff choose the new-release table personally; nothing goes on display because a publisher paid for the space. Secondhand stock is bought from readers who bring boxes in, priced by hand, and shelved by genre rather than alphabetically — we think browsing works better that way.

Who runs the shop

  • Owen Marsh — owner, mostly found in Nonfiction
  • Priya Anand — floor staff, runs the weekly staff-pick recordings
  • Cass Roy — orders and secondhand buying
  • Ferdi Nakamura — events and the monthly reading night

Our approach

"We'd rather sell you the right book slowly than the popular one quickly." — Owen, on the shop's buying philosophy

That means we ask questions before we recommend anything, and we're just as happy pointing you toward the library or a different shop if we don't have the right fit.
